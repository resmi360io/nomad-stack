## 2. The full no-sampling read (the "other 2000")

The standing complaint was that a large block of postings was never read. That block was
real. At the start of this run 2,024 postings carried no verdict, 1,996 of them
freelancer and upwork going back to 16 August, and only about 800 of those were new since
the stall. The rest had been ingested and never queued.

A fresh queue was built over every unread, non-dupe posting (1,902 rows) and every row was
read and classified. No sampling. Result for this queue:

- 783 BID (fingerprinted), 1,119 REJECT, 0 unread.

Whole-corpus state after the run:

- 11,855 postings total, 3,933 BID, the rest REJECT or dupes, 0 unread in scope.
- BID by run: v4-2026-09-01 = 1,938, v3-2026-08-25 = 1,095, v4-2026-09-02 = 783 (this run),
  earlier untagged = 117.

The BID rate on the new read tracked the source. Freelancer and upwork batches ran roughly
30 to 45 percent BID. The reddit and wwr blocks were the richest, above 65 percent BID, and
they are where the automation and integration work concentrates. Marketing, design,
content, transcription, virtual-assistant, data-collection, and employment listings were
rejected. Prohibited buckets (gambling, unregulated crypto and token issuance, binary and
OTC options bots, escort and adult listings, fake engagement and review generation, ad-view
fraud, mass email and SMS spam infrastructure, cloaking, credential and session-persistence
evasion, watermark defeat, shift-grabbing bots against platform terms) were rejected with a
specific reason and never fingerprinted.

## 3. Repetition accumulates with the observation window

The core thesis held and strengthened. Route repetition on the pooled real-source corpus
(normalized operation class, source platform, target platform, excluding degenerate
brief-only postings) now reads:

- 3,046 real-source postings across 965 routes over a 17-day window = **3.16 postings per
  route**.

That is up from the pre-read v4 measurement of 2.59 and the v3 run's 2.27. Adding the
previously unread block did not scatter into new routes, it concentrated into the routes
that were already common. Postings rose while distinct routes did not, which is exactly what
the repetition-accumulates thesis predicts. Degenerate spec and brief postings pool far
harder, 1,436 postings across 131 routes = 10.96 per route, because a bare brief carries no
system to discriminate on.

## 4. The promotion gate: half of real-source work is repeated shape

Gate: a route promotes at n >= 6 postings, >= 2 distinct feeds, and >= 4 distinct days. On
the full corpus:

- 82 routes clear the gate, covering 1,566 postings.
- That is **51 percent of all real-source work** sitting inside repeated, multi-feed,
  multi-day shapes (up from 43 percent before this read).

## 5. Price inversion holds: repeated work pays less per job, not more

- Gated (repeated) routes: median fixed budget **$200**.
- Once-only routes (n = 1): median fixed budget **$250**.

The repeated shapes pay less per individual job than the one-off work, and there is more of
it, arriving on more feeds, on more days. The money is in the reproducibility, not the
ticket size. This is the whole point of hunting shapes rather than contracts.

## 9. What changed, what held, versus v3

Held:
- Repeated shapes outnumber one-off contracts, and repetition rises as the window grows.
- Price inversion: repeated work pays less per job than one-off work.
- Files, web hosting, and documents are the universal connectors.

Changed:
- Repetition per route rose from 2.27 (v3) to 3.16 (v4 full corpus) once the unread block
  was read. This is the single clearest confirmation of the thesis this run produced.
- Gate coverage rose from 43 to 51 percent of real-source work.
- The Shopify build-and-fix pair overtook everything else as the single largest reproducible
  shape (227 postings), with generic saas_app automation glue right behind it and
  saturating fastest.
- Two feeds (hn, remotive) are confirmed dead since 18 August and need fixing before the
  next run, or the corpus will keep leaning on freelancer, upwork, and reddit.

## Method note

Fingerprints use the 14-field workflow schema, hashed over the 10 discriminating fields.
Routes are the pooling-safe unit: normalized operation class plus source platform plus
target platform. Degenerate postings (source_system in spec or brief) are pooled separately
because a client-supplied brief carries no system to discriminate on. All verdicts and
fingerprints for this run are tagged run = v4-2026-09-02 in jobs.posts. Nothing was written
to the public schema. No proposals were auto-submitted.
