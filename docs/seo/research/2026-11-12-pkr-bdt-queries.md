# PKR and BDT: what people search and ask, 2026-11-12

Scope: Pakistan (USD to PKR) and Bangladesh (USD to BDT), the topics paidacross.com covers: what a freelancer or
remote worker actually receives, which provider is cheapest, rates, fees, withdrawal routes. "PLR" in the request was
read as PKR.

Sources and limits. Our own Search Console (90 days to 2026-11-09, 134 page and query rows, mostly very small counts),
Bing Webmaster query rows (to 2026-11-06), OpenRush keyword and live SERP data for the Pakistan and Bangladesh
markets (2026-11-12), and two web searches for forum wording. The web searches returned no actual Reddit, Quora or
Facebook-group threads, only vendor blogs, guide sites and a news item, so forum wording below comes from the SERP
titles and snippets, not from reading threads. Volumes for this niche are small and Bangladesh results come back in
Bengali market data, so treat every volume as direction only (measurement rule 1). Nothing below is a verified fee
claim: any number quoted from a third party must go through the verifier and reviewer before it reaches a page.

## 2. What the market searches (keyword tool, Pakistan and Bangladesh locations)

- "payoneer" alone: 165,000 a month in Pakistan, navigational. Not winnable, but it is the demand pool.
- "paypal pakistan": 12,100 a month, navigational. Shows how many people look for a PayPal route that does not exist for
  residents. Relevant to the "Payoneer vs PayPal" question we already rank for.
- "payoneer fees": 390 a month in Pakistan, 140 in Bangladesh (US CPC about 1.5 to 1.9, so advertisers value it).
- "payoneer fees in pakistan": 90; "payoneer withdrawal fee": 110 (Pakistan); "payoneer withdrawal fees": 30 (Bangladesh).
- "payoneer to payoneer transfer fee": 260 in Pakistan, 70 in Bangladesh.
- "payoneer tax per transaction": 30; "payoneer tax in pakistan": 20.
- "fiverr commission rate": 30; "payment methods in pakistan": 110.
- The tool found no autocomplete-style long-tail for "receive dollars in pakistan freelancer" or the Bangladesh
  equivalent, so the long tail has to come from our own Search Console and Bing data, not from this tool.
- Most other hits on "rate" seeds (bank rate sheets, electricity bills, property files) are unrelated; ignore.

## 3. What Google shows for the core queries (2026-11-12)

"payoneer dollar rate today pakistan" (Pakistan):
- AI Overview present, saying Payoneer publishes no fixed daily rate, that the official rate is about 276.5 PKR, and that
  a conversion fee "can be up to 3.5%". It links to Payoneer's own pages.
- Organic: a news-style result about a reported 3% Payoneer withdrawal fee for Pakistani users, exchange-service pages
  (okchanger, xchangic Payoneer to JazzCash), a Wise comparison page, a comparison guide (pakmoneyguide.com, "How to
  Receive Dollars in Pakistan - Payoneer, Wise, and ..."), a Reddit thread titled "current exchange rate minus 3% (USD
  to PKR)" about Payoneer, a how-to video.
- People Also Ask: what is the buying rate of dollars in Pakistan, is the bank rate today, what is a good exchange rate,
  how much is 1 dollar in black market rate today.
- Related searches: Payoneer dollar rate today in rupees, PayPal USD to PKR, Apni exchange PayPal withdrawal, PayPal
  dollar rate, PayPal rate for today.

"payoneer dollar rate in bangladesh today" (Bangladesh):
- AI Overview present, citing paidacross.com's rate page twice, and giving an effective Payoneer rate of about 119 to 121
  BDT against about 124.5 official.
- Organic: dollar buy and sell services, WorldRemit, TransferGo and remittance-rate sites, Bengali-language videos on
  Payoneer to bank fees and timing, Bangla Swap (Payoneer balance exchange guide).
- People Also Ask: current dollar purchase rate in Bangladesh, best time to exchange currency, "What is Payoneer USD to
  PKR rate?" (a Pakistan question showing in Bangladesh), where to find a currency calculator.

Takeaways: (a) searchers mix three intents under one query: the official rate, what Payoneer pays, and where to sell
the balance (exchange services, wallets); (b) our BDT rate page is already cited in Google's AI Overview for its core
query, which the AI-visibility baseline did not have a month ago; (c) the SERPs are dominated by exchange services and
affiliate-style guides, whose fee claims often disagree with official pages (the bKash page states 3% plus $1 for
withdrawals to bKash; other pages quote 2% or a flat $1.50), so a sourced, dated answer is the differentiator.

## 4. Question clusters, grouped by what the page has to answer

1. What rate will Payoneer actually give me today, and how far is it from the official rate? (both countries, highest
   volume in our data; rate pages)
2. What does it cost to withdraw to my bank, and what is taken before that? (fee, conversion markup, per-withdrawal fee;
   Pakistan: reported 3% withdrawal fee story, Meezan and other bank wording; Bangladesh: bank and bKash)
3. Which route is cheapest: Payoneer vs Wise vs PayPal vs bank wire vs a local exchange service. (Bing wording; PayPal
   is not an option for Pakistan residents, which the "paypal pakistan" demand suggests many do not know)
4. How do I get USD to a wallet: JazzCash and Easypaisa in Pakistan, bKash, Nagad and OK Wallet in Bangladesh. ("payoneer to
   bkash" at 55.4 is our biggest unserved Bangladesh query)
5. Salary in USD from a foreign employer (not freelance platform): how to receive it and what it costs. (Pakistan FAQ
   shipped; Bangladesh not yet)
6. Is it legal and what about tax or the incentive: Bangladesh remittance incentive (2.5%), Pakistan filer and tax
   questions ("payoneer tax in pakistan"). Compliance claims; primary sources needed.
7. Small-amount questions: "how much will I get from 100 or 120 USD", "94 usd to pkr how much fee". Fixed fees dominate
   at small amounts; the calculator answers this.
8. Sender-side: "what's the cheapest way to pay freelancers in pakistan, india and the philippines" (pillar and sender
   page).
9. Payoneer-to-Payoneer transfer fee and minimums (260 a month in Pakistan). Adjacent, not a paidacross topic yet.

## 5a. Correction after checking existing page coverage (2026-11-12, same day)

Section 5 below was written before comparing each idea with the FAQs already live. Checked against `data/corridors.ts`
and `data/ratePages.ts`, four of the seven are already answered, so they are NOT gaps:

- Item 1 (Payoneer to bKash): the Bangladesh corridor already has "Can I receive Payoneer payments directly into bKash or
  Nagad?", "Which is better: Payoneer to bank account or to bKash?", a long "How the Payoneer-to-bKash withdrawal works"
  section, and the BDT rate page has two bKash FAQs. The live SERP for "payoneer to bkash" (Bangladesh) is dedicated
  single-topic guides, several Bengali videos and bKash's own page (which Google's AI Overview cites). Our position 55
  reflects authority and page focus, not missing content. A fourth bKash FAQ would duplicate; a dedicated page conflicts
  with the do-not-multiply-pages rule. Decision: no new copy; revisit if the corridor page's bKash section is ever split.
- Item 4 (JazzCash): the PKR rate page already has "What is the Payoneer to JazzCash dollar rate today?".
- Item 5 (official rate vs Payoneer rate): both rate pages already have "Why is the Payoneer exchange rate lower than the
  rate on Google?" and "Why is the Payoneer dollar rate lower than the bank rate?".
- Item 6 (PayPal in Pakistan): the PKR corridor already has "Is PayPal available for freelancers in Pakistan?".

Still real: item 2 (Bangladesh USD salary FAQ, no equivalent exists), item 3 (verify the reported 3% Pakistan withdrawal
fee against primary sources; it affects existing copy, not a new FAQ), and item 7 (Payoneer-to-Payoneer transfer fee,
not covered, low priority, borderline on-concept).

## 5. Gaps and candidate actions, in priority order

Each item is a proposal. Anything touching page copy goes through researcher or verifier, reviewer and humanizer; none
is approved.

1. Bangladesh: add a "How do I withdraw from Payoneer to bKash, and what does it cost?" answer. Evidence: "payoneer to
   bkash" 10 impressions at 55.4 on the corridor page, bKash's own page publishes a conversion fee, and the SERP shows
   guide sites with conflicting figures. Needs the bKash and Payoneer primary pages as sources. Strongest single gap.
2. Bangladesh: USD salary question on the corridor page (already queued as weekly FAQ #5): "receive salary in usd" 5 at
   23.2 plus the Bing sentence-style query at position 3.
3. Pakistan: a fee-specific answer for the questions that rank at 58 to 76 today ("payoneer charges in pakistan", "how
   much payoneer charge in pakistan"). FAQ #3 on the rate page already targets this; wait for it to be recrawled and
   read it after the 2026-12-07 preliminary read before adding more. Also verify the reported 3% withdrawal fee story
   against Payoneer's own fee schedule before any copy relies on it.
4. Pakistan: wallet route (JazzCash, Easypaisa) as one FAQ, since "payoneer to jazzcash exchange rate today" appears and
   a competitor page ranks for it. Check whether Payoneer to JazzCash is a supported route before writing anything.
5. Both: a short "official rate vs Payoneer rate" explainer, answer-first, since the SERPs and Google's AI Overview
   state both figures. Our rate pages already carry a modeled figure; the gap is explaining why the numbers differ.
6. Pakistan: a short "Why can't I get PayPal in Pakistan, and what are my options" answer, to capture part of the
   12,100 a month "paypal pakistan" demand and tie into the Payoneer vs PayPal question we already rank 7.7 for.
7. Both: Payoneer-to-Payoneer transfer fee and minimum, only if it can be answered from a primary source and linked to the
   receiving topic; low priority, and it drifts from the site's concept.

Not worth pursuing: bank rate sheets, electricity bills, property file rates, generic "rate" queries; the exchange
services' "buy or sell Payoneer dollars" intent (a different business, and risky to recommend); the "payoneer" brand
query.
