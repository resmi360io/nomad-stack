Collected 2026-11-18 from YouTube's public search results pages (relevance order, first page, US English
interface), not a paid tool. Columns: title | channel | length | views | age | link. View counts and ages as

## What the list says (for scripting)

- **Almost all are process tutorials, not cost answers.** "How to withdraw from Payoneer to X" in 1 to 6 minutes,
  screen recordings of the app. Hardly any shows what a payment actually becomes after fees and the rate. That is
  the gap our walk-through fills: the same task, with the number on screen and the cost explained.
- **Formats that earn views and are worth copying (format, never script):** the "live withdrawal" with a real
  transaction on screen (Digital Booster, Pakistan: 47,673 and 54,788 views); the side-by-side test of several apps
  (Ask Nuel, Nigeria: "I Tested Cleva, Geegpay & Grey", 16:05, 6,883 views); the plain "best ways to receive USD in
  {country}" explainer (Sunday Abegunde, Nigeria, 5:37).
- **Length:** tutorials run 1 to 6 minutes; the comparison videos that rank in Nigeria run 9 to 19 minutes. A walk-
  through of about 4 to 8 minutes per corridor group fits between them and meets the "4 minutes or more" finding
  for AI citations (tactic 78).
- **Language:** Bangladesh results are largely in Bangla and Pakistan's partly in Urdu. An English video competes
  with the English titles only; a Bangla or Urdu version is a separate decision (Q1 2027, translated pages).
- **Titles lead with the task and the year** ("How to withdraw money from Payoneer to bank account in Pakistan",
  "(Step by Step 2026)"). Ours should lead with the query and the number (tactic 62), for example "Payoneer to a
  Pakistani bank: what 1,000 dollars becomes (2026)".
