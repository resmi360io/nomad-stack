# Link-building agents and tools: what exists, what we borrowed, what is forbidden (2026-10-30)

Research run before building `outreach-analyst`. Stars and dates are as of 2026-10-30. Pages that could not
be opened are said to be so. The full report is in the session transcript; this file keeps what the agent
needs.

## Bottom line

No existing open-source agent does this legitimately for a site like ours. Most are built for volume
(scraped contacts, templated sequences, automated sending, directory and profile links, anchor-text plans),
which is what Google's link spam policy describes. We kept the judgement stages and dropped the throughput
ones: no contact scraping, no sending, no sequences.

## What we borrowed, and from where

| Source | What we took |
|---|---|
| `coreyhaines31/marketingskills`, `skills/link-building` (57,430 stars) | Check links are the bottleneck and that a linkable asset exists first; qualify on relevance, legitimacy and value to the target page, with authority used to sort and never to qualify; four outreach shapes (unlinked mention, broken link, list, resource page); under about 100 words, one ask; one follow-up then stop; the owner sends. Rejected from it: "link swaps are fine in moderation" and paid placements. |
| `Jasper0122/backlink-outreach-kit` | Tracker rules: `sent` needs a real send confirmation, `linked` needs a live URL and a checked `rel`; the test "could this line have been written without opening the page?"; never send two prospects the same body; a daily ceiling, not a quota; the inflated-authority checks (backlinks per referring domain above about 20, referring domains with no traffic). Rejected: its central "content exchange" (a link exchange). |
| `Talko90/seo-agentic-system`, `seo-links` command | Modes that each write a file; every number carries its source and fetch date; controllability tiers (unlinked, broken and resource pages first, cold media last). Rejected: guest posts with bio links at volume, and a score that rewards dofollow. |
| `m4mansoor/seo-agent` | Only its `verify_link` check (live page, real hyperlink, `rel`, not noindex). The rest is automated link building. |
| n8n template 20338 (journalist-request triage) and 3685 (backlink monitor) | Log every score, including drops, for tuning; caps on pitches; monthly live and `rel` re-check of earned links. Rejected: AI-drafted pitches (below). |
| Pitchbox, Respona, BuzzStream, Postaga, Hunter (workflow stages only) | Stage list (prospect, enrich, contact, personalise, sequence, track) and qualification fields. All are optimised for throughput; we keep only the judgement stages. |
| Siege Media scorecard; Seer Interactive; editorial.link | Outbound-link signals (sponsored-post pages, "Advertise", "Work with us"); "would I want this link even if SEO were not a factor"; traffic floors as sanity checks, not gates; a low inbound-to-outbound ratio as a sign a site sells links. |
| Qwoted and Featured | Qwoted: "Using AI to write your pitches is against Qwoted's Terms & Conditions" (https://intercom.help/qwoted/en/articles/14809747-can-i-use-ai-to-craft-my-pitch). Featured reports about 1 in 10 AI pitches selected against about 1 in 3 human ones. So the agent supplies a facts pack and the owner writes the pitch. |

Also checked and found nothing usable: VoltAgent and wshobson subagent collections (no link agent), CrewAI
examples (none), Make (no template), several GitHub tools that automate guest-post mail, contact-form
submission or directory submission (avoid).

## Forbidden patterns

Quotes are from Google's spam policies (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies,
updated 2026-08-28) unless another source is named.

| # | Pattern | Policy line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Paying for a followed link, sponsored post, guaranteed feature, niche edit or pay-per-result placement | "Buying or selling links for ranking purposes... Exchanging money for links, or posts that contain links" |
| 2 | Offering accounts, products, services or data for a link | "Exchanging goods or services for links" |
| 3 | Making a link the condition of a correction | Same line: the correction is a service |
| 4 | Link swaps, content exchanges, reciprocal placement | "Excessive link exchanges ("Link to me and I'll link to you")" |
| 5 | Automated profiles, web 2.0, bookmarks, comments, contact-form bots | "Using automated programs or services to create links to your site" |
| 6 | Forum, Reddit or Discord posts with optimised links or signatures | "Forum comments with optimized links in the post or signature" |
| 7 | Prescribing anchor text or anchor ratios | "links with optimized anchor text in articles, guest posts, or press releases distributed on other sites" |
| 8 | Guest posts written for the link, at volume, or AI-generated | "Creating low-value content primarily for the purposes of manipulating linking and ranking signals" |
| 9 | Directory and bookmark submission lists | "Low-quality directory or bookmark site links" |
| 10 | Embeddable fee tables or widgets with a credit link | "Keyword-rich, hidden, or low-quality links embedded in widgets that are distributed across various sites" |
| 11 | "Powered by" or footer badges on other sites | "Widely distributed links in the footers or templates of various sites" |
| 12 | Requiring a link to use our CSV or data | "Requiring a link as part of a Terms of Service, contract, or similar arrangement without allowing a third-party content owner the choice of qualifying the outbound link". Ask for attribution, never a followed link |
| 13 | PBNs or bought expired domains | "Expired domain abuse" |
| 14 | Paid advertorials on large publishers to rank | Site reputation abuse |
| 15 | Mass-generated link-bait pages | Scaled content abuse |
| 16 | Scraping Google results for prospecting | "scraping results for rank-checking purposes or other types of automated access to Google Search conducted without express permission" |
| 17 | Mass templated outreach, automated sequences, AI-written journalist pitches | No single Google line; forbidden because it is how 1 to 8 are produced at scale, by Qwoted's terms, and by CAN-SPAM opt-out duties (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) |
| 18 | Disavowing by a tool's "toxicity" or "spam score" | https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/2648487: only for spammy links that "have caused a manual action, or likely will" |
| 19 | Sock-puppet accounts or astroturfing | KNOWLEDGE.md Rejected list and platform rules |

Positive guidance the agent quotes: "Other sites linking to you is something that happens naturally over
time" and "you can overdo promoting your site and actually harm it"
(https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide); John Mueller, 2021: it is fine
to contact people and tell them you have useful content
(https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-outreach-link-building/413062/).
