## B. Primary-source checks

### B1. Row 154: Google's AI rules

**Source.** https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content, "Last updated 2026-10-01". The changelog at https://developers.google.com/search/updates dates it: "October 1 ... Updated the using generative AI content guide with information from the Search Quality Raters guidelines." The guide was first published 2025-05-21. There is no blog post.

Quoted from the guide:
- "generative models don't retrieve facts, but predict a likely sequence of words".
- "It is critical to manually factcheck and review all AI-generated content for accuracy and trustworthiness before publishing."
- "This review also applies to metadata like title elements, meta description elements, structured data" and "alternate texts for images".
- Generating "many pages without adding value for users ... may violate Google's spam policy on scaled content abuse".

The video is wrong on two words: "destroyed" (the guide says nothing about rankings) and "now" (the policy predates the update, and the guide says "may").

For us:
- `.claude/agents/corridor-verifier.md` lists claim types but never names title or metaDescription.
- Rate-page prose, including meta descriptions such as "Payoneer publishes no rate of its own", is verified by nobody (AGENTS.md).
- This is the primary-source reason for C1.2 and C1.4.

### B6. Row 155: "fact density is the most important citation factor"

- **Credible evidence for a narrower claim.** GEO (Aggarwal et al., KDD 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) rewrote source pages and measured their visibility in generated answers.
  - On its simulated GPT-3.5 engine: quotations about +41%, statistics about +33%, cited sources about +28%. Keyword stuffing was flat to negative.
  - On a 200-query Perplexity subset (file uploads, not live URLs): statistics gave about +9% on word share and +37% on the subjective score.
  - Results varied by domain.
- **What it does not show:** one benchmark on 2023 engines is not a ranking of factors on today's engines.
- **Google:** "There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary" (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features, updated 2025-12-10).
- **Verdict:** agency lore as stated. The weaker version matches what we already do. No change.
- **"Avoid linking competitors":** Google's starter guide says "Links can provide more context on a topic, both for users and search engines" (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide, updated 2025-12-10). We keep citing primary sources whoever owns them.

### B7. Row 152: `site:` and "Crawled - currently not indexed"

Google, quoted:
- `site:` "doesn't necessarily return all the URLs that are indexed", and "If a URL doesn't show in a `site:` query, use the URL Inspection tool" (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/search-operators/all-search-site, updated 2025-12-10).
- Page indexing report (https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7440203): "Crawled - currently not indexed" means "The page was crawled by Google but not indexed. It may or may not be indexed in the future; no need to resubmit this URL for crawling."
- Also from that page: "Don't expect every URL on your site to be indexed", and "all of your important pages should be indexed".

So the video's "request indexing again" is wrong. "Improve the page" is reasonable but is a content decision.
